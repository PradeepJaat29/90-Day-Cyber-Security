# Day 1 — Cybersecurity Foundations

**Challenge:** 90 Days of Cybersecurity with MyFirstHack  
**Focus:** Understanding the cybersecurity landscape and establishing a learning baseline.

## 1. What I learned

Today was primarily theory and introductory written lessons. I covered:

- **Cybersecurity:** The broader practice of protecting systems, networks, applications, and data from digital attacks and unauthorized access.
- **SOC (Security Operations Center):** A team/function that monitors security events, investigates alerts, triages potential incidents, and supports incident response.
- **GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance):** The discipline of managing security governance, identifying and assessing risk, and helping an organization meet policies, standards, and regulatory requirements.

I also learned why consistency and daily learning matter when building cybersecurity knowledge. Progress comes from regularly studying concepts, practising them, and documenting what I learn.
